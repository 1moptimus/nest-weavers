# Editing guide (for the Nest Weavers team)

Use this to update projects, photos and page text every few months. You do not need to touch code.

## Before you start
1. Ask the site owner to add your GitHub account as a collaborator with **write** access to the repository.
2. Open `https://nestweavers.com/admin` and click **Login with GitHub**. Approve the access request on the GitHub popup.

## Editing
- **Projects**: open *Projects*, pick one or click *New Project*. Fill in title, summary, location, year, category and the photos. Lower *Display order* numbers appear first. Tick *Hidden from site* to take a project off the website without deleting it.
- **Page titles and intro text** (Home, Why Us, About Us, Gallery, Contact Us): open *Page text*.
- **Photos**: upload through the image field. Use landscape JPG or PNG files, at least 1600px wide, under 5 MB. The site resizes them automatically.

## Publishing
1. Click **Save** to create a draft, then **Set status > Ready**, then **Publish**. (Ask the site owner if you do not see these options.)
2. The website rebuilds automatically. Allow about 2 minutes, then check the live site.

## If something goes wrong
- A save fails with a field error: fill in the highlighted field (most often a missing title or year).
- The change is not visible after a few minutes: hard-refresh the page, then tell the site owner.
- To undo a change: ask the site owner to revert it in the repository history.
